## Glossary
- [Doomscrolling](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/doomscrolling/) — Doomscrolling is continuing to scroll through bad or endless news and feeds long after it stops being useful, usually because the feed never gives a natural stopping point.
- [Habit stacking](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/habit-stacking/) — Habit stacking means attaching a new habit to one you already do every day — 'after I pour my morning coffee, I'll fill my water bottle' — so the old habit becomes the reminder.
- [Medication adherence](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/medication-adherence/) — Medication adherence is how closely a person's actual medicine-taking matches what they agreed with their prescriber — the right medicine, dose, time and duration.
- [Subscription creep](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/subscription-creep/) — Subscription creep is the slow build-up of recurring charges — free trials that renew, small monthly fees and quiet price increases — until they add up to far more than you'd agree to in one go.
- [Workweek (for overtime)](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/workweek-overtime/) — For U.S. overtime, a workweek is a fixed, regularly recurring 168-hour period — seven consecutive 24-hour days — chosen by the employer. It doesn't have to start on Sunday or match your pay period.
- [Go bag](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/go-bag/) — A go bag is a packed bag you can grab in minutes if you have to leave home quickly, holding the essentials to get through the first few days — water, food, light, medicine, documents and cash.
- [Progressive overload](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/progressive-overload/) — Progressive overload is gradually increasing the demand on your muscles over time — a little more weight, a few more reps, or slower, more controlled movement — so they keep a reason to adapt.
- [Urge surfing](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/urge-surfing/) — Urge surfing is a mindfulness technique for cravings: instead of fighting the urge or giving in, you notice it rise, peak and fall like a wave, without acting on it.
- [Markup vs margin](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/markup-vs-margin/) — Markup is profit as a percentage of your cost; margin is profit as a percentage of the price. A $100 job that cost you $60 has a 66.7% markup but a 40% margin.
- [Buffer time (appointments)](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/learn/what-is/buffer-time/) — Buffer time is the gap you deliberately leave after an appointment — for clean-up, notes, travel or a late start — so one overrun doesn't push your whole day late.
